# Welcome to SAOS, Ana-Maria

Words in **bold** are exactly what you will see on screen.

## 1. Your first sign-in (one sitting, about ten minutes)

Brian gives you a temporary password in person. It works for one sign-in and expires after 72 hours. SAOS also emails you a short notice with the sign-in link when the password is issued; the password itself is never in an email.

1. Go to **https://ops.sotoaccounting.com/login**. Enter your **Email** and the temporary **Password**, leave **Authenticator code or recovery code (if enrolled)** empty, and press **Sign in**.
2. The next screen shows a QR code with a **Secret** beneath it. In an authenticator app on your phone, add an account and scan the code. If the app cannot scan it, add the account by manual entry and type the Secret in instead.
3. Enter the six-digit code from the app in **Code from your authenticator** and press **Enable MFA + sign in**.
4. You see eight recovery codes, shown only this once. Keep them somewhere safe, but not on the phone that holds your authenticator. Press **I saved these**.
5. On **Account security**, enter the temporary password as **Current password**, then fill in **New password (12+ characters)** and **Confirm new password**, and press **Change password**. Do this straight away: once the authenticator is set up, the temporary password will not sign you in again.

From then on you sign in with your email, your password and the code from the app.

- **Phone not to hand?** Type a recovery code in the code field instead. Each code works once, and Brian is alerted when one is used. To get a new set, press **Issue new recovery codes** on **Account**. You need a current code from the app, and the new set replaces the old one.
- **Lost your phone?** Tell Brian. He resets your authenticator from Admin → Staff and you get an email. Your password stays the same, and you set up the authenticator again at your next sign-in.

## 2. Where your work is

You land on your queue, headed **My queue** (**My Queue** in the top bar). It lists the returns assigned to you in deadline order, with rejected returns at the top because the window to re-file is short. Press **Client packet** to open the client's page, then work the return from its **Returns** card. A return with no preparer yet is not in your queue: find the client under **Clients** and assign it (below). **My Tasks** holds your other work, such as IRS notices and follow-ups. Your top bar has **Clients, Documents, My Tasks, My Queue, E-file acks, Pipeline, SOPs, Alerts, Deliver Return, Recorder, Account**.

## 3. Working a return

The Returns card shows these five phases as a rail. Every button that moves a return asks you to confirm, or opens a box to fill in, before anything changes. Uploads are the exception: **Upload the signed engagement letter**, **Upload the signed 8879**, choosing the file on **Deliver Return** and **Upload ATX report** act as soon as you press them.

**Engage.** The client signs the engagement letter in the portal. If they signed on paper, fill in **Signed engagement letter (scan)** and **Date signed** and press **Upload the signed engagement letter**. If the return names no preparer yet, press **Assign preparer** and choose yourself; it then joins your queue. (When Brian has already assigned it to you, the rail offers **Change preparer** instead, and you skip this.) Then press **Lock estimate** (enter the **Low end (dollars)** and **High end (dollars)**), and **Schedule**.

**Prepare.** Press **Request documents**, then **Start preparation** (this needs a locked estimate and a named preparer), then **Internal review**.

**Sign.**
1. On **Deliver Return**, find the client, choose the **Tax engagement** and pick the **Final return PDF (from ATX)**. The return goes to the client's portal and moves to Client review.
2. Press **Record 8879 sent**, then choose the **Method** (Adobe Sign, In office or Mailed) and the **Date sent**. SAOS sends nothing; this only records that it went out.
3. When the wet-signed 8879 comes back, scan it. Fill in **Signed 8879 (scan)**, **Signed on** (the date of the signature), **PTIN holder** and **Form**, then press **Upload the signed 8879**. This upload is what authorizes filing. **Form** opens on the right 8879 for the return (8879-TE for a 990); leave it unless the client signed a different one.

**File.**
1. Press **Set final fee** and enter the **Final fee (dollars)**. A fee outside the quoted range needs a reason.
2. Press **Ready to file**.
3. After you transmit in ATX, press **Mark filed**. Fill in **Filed on** and **PTIN holder (the paid preparer of record)**. Under **Jurisdictions filed**, Federal is always listed. Add any states with **Add a state (two-letter code)** and **Add state**, and mark each jurisdiction E-filed or Paper (mailed). Mark filed is refused until the signed 8879 is on file.
4. The ATX report (section 4) answers e-filed jurisdictions. For each paper one, press the rail's button for that state (for example **Record mailing — IL**), enter **Mailed on**, **Method** and **Tracking number**, and press **Record mailing**.

**Close.** Filing issues the final-fee invoice, and the client pays it in the portal. The return completes by itself once every jurisdiction has been accepted or mailed.

**Extensions:** press **Record extension** and enter the **Extension form** and **Date filed**. The form opens on the right one for the return (8868 for a 990). SAOS works out the new deadline.

## 4. The ATX report

1. In ATX, export the E-Files list as CSV. On **E-file acks**, press **Upload ATX report**. Nothing is sent at this point.
2. **Open** the report and review the matched rows:
   - A rejection becomes a task for you.
   - A pending row needs nothing.
   - An extension row has its own **Record extension** button.
   - Rows that are not our returns are listed as unmatched and do nothing.
   - Press **Hold** on anything that should not go out.
3. Press **Release N to clients**, then **Release N**. Each accepted client is emailed in their language. That email is switched on; if Brian ever switches it off, the rows are recorded as held and nothing goes out.

## 5. "Not available to your role"
